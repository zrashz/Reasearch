# Annotation guide v0.1 — pilot draft, not frozen

An important finding is paper-supported, relevant to the news topic and necessary for the main result or a material qualification. Dates, IDs and incidental background numbers are not automatically findings.

Keep pair eligibility separate from finding inventories. Use original paper evidence to review code-assisted candidates; check for omitted findings. Record atomic statements, value/unit/population/condition/comparison/uncertainty, evidence ID/location, importance reason and acceptable tolerances. Tolerances are currently undecided; do not invent a universal numeric tolerance.

Mark where support is available under each evidence condition. A news statement is topical guidance, not admissible paper support. In E1, exclude table/figure captions and derived records. Rejecting a claim must not trigger substitution with an unrelated finding.

Inventories must be independent of final generated outputs and fixed before final tests. Seek independent second review of at least 25% of validation and test cases; this is a proposed minimum, not a proven reliability threshold. Define the review unit, selection process and agreement procedure before annotation. Record independent decisions before adjudication. Without a reviewer, disclose single-annotator exploratory evaluation.
